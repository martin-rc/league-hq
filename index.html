What is luck here?

Every week we pretend you played all 7 other teams. Beat 5 of them and that week is worth 5/7 = 0.71 "deserved" wins. Add up every week and you get the wins your scores earned. Luck = actual wins − deserved wins.

🍀 Lucky win: you won with a below-median score (your opponent laid an egg). 💀 Unlucky loss: you lost with an above-median score (you ran into a buzzsaw).

How locking works, and how it's graded

A slot scores its player's latest game of the week unless you lock it. Every lock is a bet that the score you have beats what's still to come.

🔒 Lock gain: the locked score minus what that player's last game of the week would have given you if you'd ridden it out. Hit rate: share of locks that beat riding it out. Lock efficiency: your real points ÷ what you'd have scored if every starter had locked his best game of the week (perfect hindsight, so 100% is impossible). Lock bar: the median score a manager locks at, where they usually pull the trigger.

Built from real lineups exported from sleeper.com: every weekly total matches Sleeper's official record. Locks on a player's final game of the week don't count as decisions.

How the numbers work
  • Records, points for and against are Sleeper's official numbers.
  • Weekly scores and every slot score come from real lineups exported from sleeper.com, with each player's box scores scored with the league's settings. Every weekly total matches Sleeper's official record; 8 of 392 team-weeks have a slot that couldn't be pinned down exactly.
  • 2024 week 2 was voided (every game a tie, not counted in points), so it's left out of weekly stats.
  • Draft value compares a pick's average fantasy points a game that season with a curve fitted to what each slot usually returns (20+ games).
  • Profile pictures are each manager's Sleeper avatar, saved Nov 4 2026.
